What we offer

Code-mixed Telugu understanding

Trained with Telugu–English code-mixed data so you can chat naturally, the way you text on WhatsApp.

Knowledge-base powered (RAG)

Built using a medical text knowledge base, multilingual sentence transformers, and FAISS vector search for grounded answers.

GPT-2 + LoRA fine-tuning

GPT-2 small with lightweight LoRA adapters tuned for health dialogues in Telugu and English mix.

Safe & informational only

Designed for guidance and education — not a substitute for professional diagnosis, treatment, or emergency care.

How Aarogyamitra works

1

Ask in Telugu or code-mix

Type your question in pure తెలుగు, English, or both. The bot handles casual, mixed-script queries.

2

Retrieve from KB

A multilingual sentence-BERT model finds the closest medical snippets from the knowledge base using FAISS.

3

Generate a grounded reply

GPT-2 with LoRA fine-tuning uses those snippets to answer in friendly, easy-to-understand Telugu.

About Aarogyamitra

Aarogyamitra is a research prototype exploring how Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) and small language models can support health information access for Telugu speakers. All responses are automatically generated and should be verified with a qualified medical professional.
