Fever (Jwaram)
Fever ante body lo infection or viral flu valla ostundi. Mundu panic avvakandi. Body pain, weakness, chills or headache unte rest mandatory. 
✔ Warm water frequently tagandi to keep body hydrated.
✔ Light food like kanji, fruits, dal rice tinetappudu stomach easy ga digest avutundi.
✔ Paracetamol 500mg only if fever high unte and doctor recommend chesunte matrame.
✔ Sponging chesthe temperature slow ga takkuvavutundi.
🚫 Ice water, cold drinks avoid cheyyandi.
⚠️ 103°F kanna ekkuva fever 24 hours kanna ekkuva unte or chest pain, confusion unte immediate ga hospital ki vellandi.

Headache (Talaneoppi)
Headache dehydration, tension, cold or lack of sleep valla ostundi. 
✔ Silent room lo rest teesukondi and mobile, TV avoid cheyyandi.
✔ Slight forehead massage and warm water tagadam help chestundi.
✔ Eye strain unte screen time tagginchandi and proper sleep techukondi.
✔ Paracetamol okka sari doctor suggest chesunte tisukovachu.
🚫 Loud noise, bright light avoid cheyyandi.
⚠️ Headache toh vomiting, vision problem, numbness unte emergency medical help teesukondi.

Cough (Dummu / Cough)
Cough ante throat irritation or chest infection valla ostundi. 
✔ Warm water and steam inhalation 5–10 minutes help chestundi.
✔ Honey + warm water mild cough ki super relief isthundi (Children <1 year ki honey ivvakandi).
✔ Dust and cold exposure avoid cheyyandi.
✔ Cough medication doctor suggest chesunte matrame use cheyyandi.
🚫 Smoking or cold drinks avoid cheyyandi.
⚠️ 1 week kanna ekkuva cough continue ayithe or chest pain, breathing problem unte doctor ni kalavandi.

Cold / Flu (Jwaram + Sneezing + Nose Block)
Common cold viral infection valla vastundi and usually 3–5 days lo settle avutundi. 
✔ Rest and warm fluids like soup, tea, turmeric milk helpful.
✔ Nose block unte saline drops use chesthe free ga breathe chestaru.
✔ Steam inhalation twice a day chesthe congestion thaggutundi.
🚫 Ice creams, fridge water avoid cheyyandi.
⚠️ High fever, severe cough, chest tightness unte medical help teesukondi.

Throat Pain / Sore Throat (Gantuloppi)
Throat pain cold, infection or voice strain valla ostundi.
✔ Warm salt water gargle twice a day cheyyandi.
✔ Warm water tagadam and spicy food avoid cheyyadam useful.
✔ Honey + turmeric mixed with warm milk throat soothing relief isthundi.
🚫 Ice water avoid cheyyandi.
⚠️ Severe pain, swallowing difficulty or fever unte doctor ni kalavandi.

Body Pain / Joint Pain (Elder Friendly)
Age valla calcium and joint lubrication taggadam valla pain vastundi.
✔ Warm compress or mild hot water bath relief isthundi.
✔ Light stretching or walking helpful.
✔ Calcium foods like milk, curd, nuts tinandi.
✔ If pain severe unte doctor suggest chesina painkiller use cheyyandi.
⚠️ Sudden swelling, redness or fever unte doctor consult compulsory.

BP (Blood Pressure)
High BP silent disease — symptoms lekunda untundi kani serious ga tisukovali.
✔ Salt thagginchandi and deep breathing daily practice cheyyandi.
✔ Walking 20–30 min chesi stress control cheyyandi.
✔ Tablets doctor prescribe chesunte skip cheyyakandi.
⚠️ Severe headache, blurred vision, nose bleeding unte emergency care.

Sugar/Diabetes
Blood sugar control maintain cheyyadam important.
✔ White rice thagginchi ragi, brown rice, oats try cheyyandi.
✔ Fruits ok but moderation lo.
✔ Morning walk and sleep regular maintain cheyyandi.
🚫 Sweets, soft drinks avoid cheyyandi.
⚠️ Dizziness, weakness, sweating unte sugar low — quick sweet item tinandi.
